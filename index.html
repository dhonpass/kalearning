💙 About Us

Kalearning is made with love for children under 10. We believe learning should be colorful, playful, and safe.

We are dedicated to creating meaningful learning experiences for children through technology. Our mission is to make learning simple, engaging, and culturally meaningful for young learners. This project was started by Ka Learning Team in 2014. The positive response from parents inspired us to build educational apps that help children learn language, culture, and basic knowledge in a fun way.

The GOAL of Ka Learning is to make learning joyful for children while helping them connect with their language and culture. With a child-friendly design and interactive style, it encourages young learners to explore Tibetan letters step by step playfully.
